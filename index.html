Our Sustainability Journey

Milestones in Intel's evolution from foundational computing breakthroughs to operational sustainability commitments.

Click a card to expand the milestone.

  1. 1968 Intel Founded Intel is founded, beginning its journey as a leader in technology and innovation.
  2. 1971 First Microprocessor Intel releases the world's first commercial microprocessor, transforming computing forever.
  3. 1978 x86 Architecture The 8086 processor launches, establishing the x86 platform used in modern computers.
  4. 1985 32-Bit Computing Intel introduces the 386 processor, bringing major advances in PC performance.
  5. 2006 Emissions Peak Intel reaches its highest operational greenhouse gas emissions and begins major sustainability investments.
  6. 2020 RISE Initiative Intel launches its RISE strategy, setting ambitious sustainability goals for 2030.
  7. 2022 Net-Zero Commitment Intel announces plans to achieve net-zero operational greenhouse gas emissions by 2040.
  8. 2023 99% Renewable Power Intel reaches 99% renewable electricity usage across its global operations.
  9. 2024 Sustainability Summit Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit to advance collaboration on sustainable manufacturing.
